What is Pocket Play?

A small collection of calm, cheerful games you can hand to your child on your phone, without worrying about what they'll tap next.

  • Made for kids

    Big buttons, bright colours and almost no reading. There's no losing, so it's never frustrating.

  • No ads

    No ads, no purchases and no links out of the app. Nothing to tap that leads somewhere else.

  • No competitions

    No leaderboards, timers or lives to lose. Kids play at their own pace, just for fun.

  • No accounts

    No sign-up and no tracking. Nothing about your child ever leaves your phone.

  • Favourite games, made simple

    Simplified versions of the games you know, sized for small fingers.

Tip: add Pocket Play to your home screen and it works offline, even in airplane mode.
